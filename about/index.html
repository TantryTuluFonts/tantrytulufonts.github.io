Prahlad Prasad Tantry

Prahlad Prasad Tantry is an author and type designer who works on the Tulu language and its script. He wrote Tuḷu Varṇāmr̥ta, a primer that teaches the traditional Tuḷu script and proposes a standard for writing Tuḷu, and he designed every font published by the Tantry Tulu Font Foundry, along with the typing standard and the keyboard that go with them.

His aim is simple: keep the traditional script exactly as it is, and make it easy to read, write and type in a modern world, with tools that anyone can use.

I have been learning the Tuḷu script since the eighth grade. Growing up outside Tuḷunāḍụ kindled my curiosity about the script and its history, and I began piecing together what I could from documents, books, manuscripts, fonts, and whatever other sources I could find. Along the way, I began making fonts and other tools to make the script easier to use.

Prahlad Prasad Tantry, from the back cover of Tuḷu Varṇāmr̥ta

What he has made

Tuḷu Varṇāmr̥ta

The book: the whole traditional script, and a standard for writing Tuḷu.

Five Tulu fonts

Darage, Mandara, Allige, Mallige and the Unicode font Mallige Unicode.

AccTantra

The typing standard that makes every ASCII Tulu font interchangeable.

A Tulu keyboard

A Keyman keyboard for typing Tulu-Tigalari as Unicode.
